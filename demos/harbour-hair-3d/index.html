The real Harbour Hair

The photographs behind this little world.

Harbour Hair is at Unit 15, Central Arcade, Leeds LS1 6DX. This 3D version takes its character from the salon photographs, with the layout updated from the owner's description: a gently inset glazed entrance continuing to the wash divider, a smaller recessed toilet behind a white screen wall, a small washing sink, three compact shampoo loungers with integrated basins, white-striped reception and colour cubbies open on both sides. The colour bar occupies roughly 80% of the main width. The remaining side passage has two steps up to styling, with a circular coat rack beside the single glass step near the entrance. Dimensions and areas not shown in the photos remain approximate.

Explore the salon in daylight, golden hour or after hours with the lighting controls.

Sources: Harbour Hair website / Harbour Hair on Treatwell

Salon imagery belongs to its respective owners. This is an interior concept, not an architectural survey.
